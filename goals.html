Goals

My long-term goal is to be a software engineer building intelligent systems that run in the real world: on devices, in robots, at the edge, where code meets people and physical things.

Where I want to be

Ten years from now, I want to lead engineering on products where machine learning, software, and hardware come together: embedded AI, robotics, and edge computing. These are systems that make decisions in milliseconds, on limited power, for people who will never see the code.

Computer Engineering is the right foundation for that. A pure software path would teach me to write the model; CompE also teaches me what happens when the model meets a sensor, a processor, and a motor. I want to be the engineer who understands the full stack and can work across those boundaries.

In the near term, I'm targeting software engineering internships that build strong fundamentals in scalable systems, clean code, and shipping fast, while I keep developing the hardware and ML skills that will set me apart later.

How I get from here to there

  1. Master the fundamentals

    Build a rigorous base in data structures and algorithms, digital design, linear algebra, and signals, and finish on Georgia Tech's accelerated three-year track by May 2028 while keeping a 4.0.

  2. Specialize with intent

    Use CompE threads in computing hardware and signal and information processing to build depth in embedded systems and data-driven computing, then add upper-level courses in embedded design and machine learning.

  3. Ship in industry

    Turn my startup SWE experience into internships at larger engineering organizations each summer, focused on production software, cloud infrastructure, and ML systems.

  4. Build in public

    Keep adding new projects to this portfolio, joining a Vertically Integrated Project team for long-term research, and pushing my work to GitHub.

  5. Lead a capstone

    Use the two-semester senior design capstone to lead an end-to-end intelligent hardware/software product, then launch into a full-time software engineering role.
